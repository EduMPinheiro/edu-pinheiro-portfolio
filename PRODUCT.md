## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React + Vite for a fast static SPA with expressive, accessible motion. Deployment is undecided; validate locally first.

## Users

Potential clients looking for a freelancer who can design and develop a personalized website, landing page, prototype, or redesign. They need to understand the offer quickly, see credible work, and start a conversation through WhatsApp or email.

## Product Purpose

Edu Pinheiro's personal portfolio presents selected web design and development work, making the freelancer's range, authorship, and contact path immediately clear. Success means a first-time visitor understands the offer within seconds, can distinguish client, concept, and independent work, and can start a conversation without a form.

## Positioning

Edu Pinheiro builds digital products as an individual web designer and developer, combining visual direction with implementation instead of presenting a generic agency or an automated template service.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is a bilingual, mobile-first web experience. The initial language follows the browser language: Portuguese for `pt-*`, English for other languages. Visitors can switch manually. The site uses live project links and locally captured project previews as evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home with four featured projects and three complementary projects.
- Individual case pages for Instituto Nastri, Beth Gauna, Zarah Flor, and Tarot da Joli.
- Project tags: Conceito / Cliente / Autoral in Portuguese; Concept / Client / Independent in English.
- Direct contact through WhatsApp and email; no mandatory form or calendar.
- Include GitHub, Instagram `@edu_mpinheiro`, and São Paulo in the footer.

- Instituto Nastri, Zarah Flor, and Rafael Montenegro are concepts; Sem Panelinha and Dex+ are independent projects; Beth Gauna and Tarot da Joli are client projects.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before explaining the process.
- Make authorship and project status explicit.
- Use visual ambition without making basic navigation or contact difficult.
- Treat the portfolio as an experience, not a corporate brochure.
- Let every animation clarify continuity, hierarchy, or interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain fully usable with keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, semantic links, alt text for project imagery, and reduced-motion behavior. Hover cannot be the only way to discover project information.
